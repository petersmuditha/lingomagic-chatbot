LINGOMAGIC - FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

ABOUT LINGOMAGIC
LingoMagic is an online language learning platform founded in 2020.
We offer courses in English, Spanish, French, German, and Japanese.
Our method combines interactive lessons, native-speaker tutors, and AI-powered conversation practice.

SUBSCRIPTION PLANS
We offer three subscription tiers:
- Basic: 9.99 EUR/month. Access to all interactive lessons and AI conversation practice.
- Premium: 24.99 EUR/month. Includes 4 live lessons per month with a native tutor.
- Unlimited: 49.99 EUR/month. Unlimited live lessons, priority support, and certification exams included.
All plans include a 7-day free trial. Cancel anytime, no questions asked.

HOW TO CANCEL A SUBSCRIPTION
To cancel your subscription, go to Account Settings > Billing > Cancel Subscription.
You will keep access until the end of your current billing period.
Refunds are available within 14 days of purchase if you have used less than 2 hours of lessons.

PAYMENT METHODS
We accept credit cards (Visa, Mastercard, American Express), PayPal, and bank transfers.
Payments are processed securely via Stripe. We never store your card details on our servers.
Invoices are available for download in Account Settings > Billing > Invoices.

CERTIFICATIONS
LingoMagic certifications are recognized by over 200 companies across Europe.
To obtain a certificate, you must complete the final exam of your course with a score of at least 80%.
Certificates are issued digitally and can be shared on LinkedIn.
Premium and Unlimited subscribers can take the exam for free. Basic subscribers pay a 29 EUR fee.

FINDING A TUTOR
All tutors on LingoMagic are native speakers and hold a teaching certification (TEFL, CELTA, or equivalent).
You can browse tutors by language, availability, and specialty (business, exam prep, conversation).
Book a trial lesson with any tutor for free before committing.

TECHNICAL ISSUES
If you cannot access a lesson, try:
1. Refreshing the page or clearing your browser cache.
2. Checking your internet connection (minimum 5 Mbps recommended).
3. Trying a different browser (Chrome, Firefox, or Safari).
4. Disabling browser extensions that might interfere with video calls.
If the issue persists, contact support at support@lingomagic.com.

MOBILE APP
The LingoMagic app is available for iOS (App Store) and Android (Google Play).
You can download lessons for offline use with a Premium or Unlimited subscription.
Progress syncs automatically between the app and the website.
